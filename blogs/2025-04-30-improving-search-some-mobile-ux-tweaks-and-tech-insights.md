April was all about getting data in and out of the Catalog. We improved search by introducing a new search engine, tweaked the mobile UX of Roadie to make it more usable (at 4am when you need to grab some techdocs for an incident), and extended the limits on historical data ingestion for Tech Insights. Oh, and introduced the DX plugin to make syncing data between DX <> Roadie easier.
